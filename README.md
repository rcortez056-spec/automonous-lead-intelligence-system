# Autonomous Lead Intelligence System

A self-serve, multi-agent AI blueprint built on Relevance AI to autonomously research, score, and generate personalized outreach for B2B leads — in seconds.

## Live Demo

[Launch Live Demo](https://ramon-lead-system.netlify.app)

## What It Does

Drop a lead in. The agent researches the company, scores intent, surfaces pain points, and writes a personalized email — in seconds.

**Input:** First Name + Email + Company/Domain → hit **Run Intelligence**

**Pipeline:** Research → Analyze → Score → Email

## Agent Tasks

1. **Research & Analysis** — Extracts core operations, scale, and primary pain points for the provided company
2. **Lead Scoring** — Evaluates lead quality on a 0-100 scale based on fit, operational complexity, and automation potential. Labels: HOT LEAD (80-100), WARM LEAD (50-79), COLD LEAD (0-49)
3. **Email Generation** — Drafts a concise, personalized outreach email addressing their specific operational pain points with a low-friction CTA

## Output

- Lead Score & Status
- Company Summary
- Top 3 Operational Pain Points
- Generated Email (Subject Line + Body)
- Recommended Action
